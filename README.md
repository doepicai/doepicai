## Featured project: Wokok Box

Formerly Wokok VPN. Your own VPN, tunnels, deploys, tracking checks and scam protection in one app. Everything runs on your device and your own server: no accounts, no backend, no telemetry. MIT licensed.

> Early release: the new features are still being tested.
