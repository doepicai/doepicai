## AybBen

Modern Armenian in short mini-games: Mashtots' alphabet, everyday words and phrases, audio and a spaced-repetition dictionary. In English and Russian. Free, with no ads and no sign-up, and it works offline.

| Android, Windows | [latest release](https://github.com/doepicai/aybben-site/releases/latest) |

## Wokok Box

Formerly Wokok VPN. Your own VPN, tunnels, deploys, tracking checks and scam protection in one app. It works with your device and your own server: no accounts, no backend, no telemetry. MIT licensed.

> Early release: the new features are still being tested.

| | |
|---|---|
| Website | [wokok.doepic.ai](https://wokok.doepic.ai) |
| Windows, macOS, Android, iOS | [arsenikt/WokokVPN-App](https://github.com/arsenikt/WokokVPN-App) ([latest release](https://github.com/arsenikt/WokokVPN-App/releases/latest)) |
| Chrome extension | [arsenikt/WokokVPN-Chrome](https://github.com/arsenikt/WokokVPN-Chrome) ([latest release](https://github.com/arsenikt/WokokVPN-Chrome/releases/latest)) |
